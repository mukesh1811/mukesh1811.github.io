Why I built F.R.I.D.A.Y

My contrarian opinions

  • Self-serve is a myth. Execs still ask ad hoc analyses despite smarter BI tools
  • Empowering Data Professionals is the way to go. Not autonomous, agentic, generative business intelligence solutions
  • Data pros need an intelligent assistant which understands the data semantics, business context and can assist thorugh discovery at the speed of thought.

What is F.R.I.D.A.Y

  • A Gen A.I. powered Analytics Assistant. Assists you co-discover insights and validate your Hypothesis from tabular data. Fast.
  • A suite of specialist agents that can perform wide variety of autonomous tasks like query your data, generate python code, create visualizations etc... orchestrated by an intelligent assistant whose only goal is Insights Co-discovery
  • Not just a reactive SQL QnA bot. It primarily understands your goal and gives proactive suggestions and recommendations
