The Problem: Self-Serve Analytics Is Failing

Despite smarter BI tools, executives still request ad hoc analyses. Data professionals are stuck in a cycle of reactive reporting instead of proactive insights discovery.

  • Self-serve is a myth - execs still ask for ad hoc analyses
  • Data teams spend more time on reports than insights
  • Current tools don't understand business context
  • No co-discovery process for data professionals

The Contrarian View: Empower Data Professionals, Not Autonomous Agents

While everyone is building autonomous, agentic, generative business intelligence solutions, we believe the real opportunity is empowering data professionals with intelligent assistance.

  • Data pros need an intelligent assistant that understands data semantics and business context
  • Co-discovery at the speed of thought, not replacement
  • Human expertise + AI assistance > Pure automation
  • Focus on insights discovery, not just query answering
