Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

90KEPT is a personal promise and daily check-in app. For privacy questions or data requests, contact mukesh1811@gmail.com.

Information we handle

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account identifier and basic profile information, such as your name, email address, and profile image where available. 90KEPT stores your account identifier, name, and email to identify your account.

The app also stores the promise you enter, your three tracking labels, timezone, daily yes/no check-ins, and associated dates and timestamps. When payments are enabled, it records purchase identifiers, payment email, and access status. The checkout provider (Razorpay, Stck, or Lemon Squeezy) processes payment information; 90KEPT does not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, save and display your promise and check-ins, apply the daily deadline in your timezone, manage purchased access, and respond to support requests. We do not request access to your Google email messages, contacts, calendar, or files. We do not sell your Google user data or use it for advertising or training AI models.

Service providers and storage

Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, application hosting, and database services. GitHub Pages serves the public landing page. Razorpay, Lemon Squeezy, or Stck provides payment services when its checkout is enabled. Razorpay and Stck purchases are matched to your Google email and purchase ID through manual seller verification before access is granted. These providers process information needed to provide their services and may collect technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under their own policies. Our application and Firestore database are hosted in Mumbai, India; other provider services may process information in other countries.

Cookies and browser storage

The app uses a session cookie and Firebase browser storage to maintain sign-in. The current app does not include advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

Retention and your requests

We retain account, promise, and check-in records to provide your account and its history. Email mukesh1811@gmail.com to request a copy of your data or account deletion. We may need to confirm that you own the account before acting on a request. Records required for legal, accounting, or security purposes may need to be retained. Deleting an account does not create an entitlement to a refund.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our data practices change.
